# Step 2: Legal Entity Registration Checklist

**Recommended structure:** Single Member Private Limited Company (Pvt Ltd) via SECP eServices
**Why Pvt Ltd over sole proprietorship:** Personal liability protection, required for future investment, cleaner for IP ownership, credibility with PSX and Capital Stake licensing discussions.

---

## Pre-Registration Decisions (do these first)

- [ ] **Choose a company name.** Must be unique — check availability at https://eservices.secp.gov.pk/eServices/
  - Shortlist 3 options in case your first choice is taken
  - Avoid names containing "bank", "exchange", "fund", "investment advisor" — these are regulated terms under SECP
  - Good framing: analytics, insights, capital, tech, fintech — e.g., "[Brand] Analytics (Private) Limited"
- [ ] **Decide registered office address.** Must be a physical address in Pakistan. Can be your home address initially.
- [ ] **Decide director(s).** For a Single Member Pvt Ltd, you are the sole director and shareholder. You need your CNIC.

---

## SECP eServices Registration

**Portal:** https://eservices.secp.gov.pk/eServices/
**Estimated time:** 1–3 business days if all documents are in order
**Estimated cost:** PKR 5,000–15,000 in government fees

- [ ] Create an account on SECP eServices
- [ ] Reserve your company name (Form-A equivalent online)
- [ ] Upload required documents:
  - CNIC copy (director)
  - Passport-size photograph (director)
  - Proof of registered office (utility bill or rent agreement)
- [ ] Pay the incorporation fee online
- [ ] Download and save your Certificate of Incorporation once issued
- [ ] Download your Memorandum and Articles of Association (MOA/AOA)

---

## Post-Incorporation (within 30 days)

- [ ] **NTN registration** — register the company with FBR at https://iris.fbr.gov.pk to get a National Tax Number. Required to open a business bank account.
- [ ] **Open a business bank account.** Bring: Certificate of Incorporation, MOA/AOA, NTN, CNIC. Recommended: Meezan Bank (if Shariah-compliant operations desired), HBL, or UBL for startup-friendliness.
- [ ] **Register for Sales Tax** (only required once annual turnover exceeds PKR 10M — skip until then)
- [ ] **Professional Tax** — register with your provincial revenue authority if applicable

---

## Trademark (Optional at this stage, recommended within 6 months)

- [ ] Conduct a trademark search at https://ipo.gov.pk/trademark-search
- [ ] File for your brand name + logo under Class 36 (financial services) and Class 42 (software/SaaS)
- [ ] Approximate cost: PKR 3,500 per class, per application
- [ ] Timeline: 12–18 months for full registration; the filing date is what protects you from day one

---
